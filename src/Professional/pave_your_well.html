Prevent Injuries During Sports

Follow these essential steps to stay safe during training

🏋️

Proper Warm-Up

Start every training session with 10-15 minutes of proper warm-up to prepare your muscles and joints. Do dynamic exercises that increase blood flow.

💧

Continuous Hydration

Drink water regularly before, during, and after training. Dehydration affects performance and injury prevention.

😴

Adequate Sleep

Get 7-9 hours of sleep at night. Good sleep helps muscle recovery and prevents fatigue-related injuries.

⚖️

Rest and Recovery

Don't train intensely every day. Take one or two complete rest days every week and gradually reduce training intensity.

🧘

Stretching and Flexibility

Practice stretching exercises regularly to maintain muscle and joint flexibility. Do this after every training session.

⚡

Gradual Progression

Increase training intensity gradually. Avoid sudden increases in intensity or duration that may cause injuries.

The Perfect Nutrition System for Athletes

Correct Nutrition = Better Performance and Injury Prevention

🍗

Proteins

Importance: Build and repair muscles

Amount: 1.2-2 grams per kilogram of body weight

Good Sources:

  • Chicken and turkey
  • Fish and seafood
  • Eggs
  • Dairy products (yogurt, cheese)
  • Legumes and lentils
  • Healthy fats: nuts and seeds
🍞

Carbohydrates

Importance: Primary energy source

Amount: 5-10 grams per kilogram depending on training

Good Sources:

  • Brown rice
  • Oatmeal
  • Whole grain bread
  • Whole wheat pasta
  • Fruits and vegetables
  • Sweet potatoes
🥑

Healthy Fats

Importance: Healthy inflammation and heart health

Amount: 20-30% of daily calories

Good Sources:

  • Olive oil
  • Avocado
  • Nuts (almonds, walnuts)
  • Seeds (flax, chia)
  • Fatty fish
  • Coconut oil
🥗

Vitamins and Minerals

Importance: Energy support and recovery

Important for Athletes:

  • Iron: Oxygen transport in blood
  • Calcium: Bone and muscle health
  • Magnesium: Muscle relaxation
  • Zinc: Immunity and recovery
  • Vitamin D: Calcium absorption
  • Vitamin C: Immune strength and healing

Essential Exercises to Improve Athletic Performance

Exercises designed to improve strength, flexibility, and endurance

💪 Strength Training

Push-ups

Benefits: Strengthen chest, shoulders, and arms

How to:

  1. Lie on your stomach with your body supported by hands and toes
  2. Lower your body until your chest nearly touches the ground
  3. Push yourself back up
  4. Repeat 3 sets of 10-15 reps

Squats

Benefits: Strengthen legs, glutes, and joints

How to:

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Slowly lower your body as if sitting on a chair
  3. Keep your back straight and balance stable
  4. Repeat 3 sets of 12-15 reps

Deadlifts

Benefits: Strengthen back, legs, and overall power

How to:

  1. Lift weights from the floor in a straight movement
  2. Keep your back straight and lean slightly forward
  3. Lower the weights the same way
  4. Repeat 3 sets of 6-10 reps

Leg Press

Benefits: Strengthen legs and quadriceps

How to:

  1. Sit on the leg press machine
  2. Set appropriate weights
  3. Push the weights using leg strength
  4. Repeat 3 sets of 10-12 reps

🧘 Flexibility and Stretching

Quadriceps Stretch

Benefits: Improve leg flexibility and prevent injuries

How to:

  1. Stand on one leg and bring the other behind you
  2. Hold your foot and push the thigh forward slightly
  3. Hold this position for 30 seconds
  4. Repeat 3 times on each side

Hamstring Stretch

Benefits: Flexibility and prevent back pain

How to:

  1. Sit on the floor with legs extended in front
  2. Gently bend forward and try to touch your toes
  3. Hold the position 30-45 seconds
  4. Repeat 3 times

Chest Stretch

Benefits: Improve posture and comfort

How to:

  1. Put one arm across your body in front
  2. Use the other arm to pull your arm towards you
  3. Hold for 30 seconds on each side
  4. Repeat 3 times

Back Relaxation

Benefits: Prevent lower back pain

How to:

  1. Lie on your back and pull your knees toward your chest
  2. Hold this position for 30 seconds
  3. You can rock your body slightly side to side
  4. Repeat 3 times

⚡ Endurance and Cardio

Running / Jogging

Benefits: Improve heart and lung capacity and overall endurance

Program:

  1. Start with 20-30 minutes of moderate running 3 times a week
  2. Keep your heart rate at about 60-70% of maximum
  3. Gradually increase distance each week
  4. You can alternate between speeds (HIIT)

Swimming

Benefits: Full body workout without joint stress

Program:

  1. Do 30-45 minutes of continuous swimming 2-3 times a week
  2. Vary between different strokes (breaststroke, butterfly, backstroke)
  3. Take gradual rest periods
  4. Swimming is excellent for injury recovery

Cycling

Benefits: Improve endurance while reducing joint stress

Program:

  1. Do 40-60 minutes of continuous cycling 3 times a week
  2. Choose different terrain (flat, uphill)
  3. Maintain consistent heart rate
  4. Gradually increase intensity

Jump and Movement (HIIT)

Benefits: Rapid improvement in strength, endurance, and calorie burn

Program:

  1. 30 seconds of intense work + 30 seconds of rest
  2. Exercises like jumping, rope climbing, squats
  3. Repeat 8-10 rounds
  4. Only once or twice a week

Friday - HIIT or Advanced Training

No training - Recovery day
