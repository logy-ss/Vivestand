01 · WHY✏️ Getting hurt is easy. Getting back is confusing.

The problem المشكلة

✏️ Hurt, and lost about recovery

✏️ Athletes who get injured often don't know how serious it is, how long it takes to heal, or which doctor to see. Advice is spread across coaches, clinics and random websites, and new recovery gear is expensive.

Our solution الحل

✏️ One place to recover

✏️ Vivestand puts a recovery plan for 20 injuries, 61 Egyptian sports doctors, a free first online session and second-hand gear on one website.
