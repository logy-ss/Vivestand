01 · The problem المشكلة

Getting hurt is easy. Getting back is confusing.

What's wrong with me?

Athletes don't know the symptoms, how serious an injury is, or how many weeks it takes to heal.

Which doctor?

It is hard to find a sports doctor for your exact injury, and a first visit costs time and money.

Gear costs too much

New braces, boots and recovery gear are expensive, while good used gear sits unused at home.
